# Schedule: Luna Arts and Sensory

When work happens. These are sensible, best-practice defaults so your Marketing OS runs on a rhythm from day one. Adjust the days and times to suit you, then keep them here so the AI follows your cadence.

## Publishing rhythm

A reliable, sustainable cadence beats a burst followed by silence (consistency is what the platforms and your audience reward).

- **Primary platform:** 3 to 5 posts per week
- **Secondary platform:** 2 to 3 posts per week
- **Newsletter:** weekly or fortnightly
- **Repurposing:** turn each anchor piece (newsletter, long video, or pillar post) into 3 to 8 smaller pieces across the week

Start at the lower end and lift it only once it is comfortably repeatable.

## Daily rhythm

- **Morning:** draft the day's content from the calendar; surface anything that needs your approval
- **Midday:** check engagement and replies; draft responses for your review
- **End of day:** a short summary of what was drafted, scheduled, and published

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday:** plan the week against your content pillars and any live campaign
- **Midweek:** prep and draft ahead so nothing is rushed
- **Friday:** review what shipped, what landed, and what to carry into next week

## Quarterly rhythm

- **Kick-off:** set three marketing outcomes for the quarter
- **Mid-quarter:** review progress and adjust
- **Close:** record what worked, retire what did not, and plan the next quarter

Everything on this schedule is drafted for your approval per `governance.md` until you choose to raise the autonomy on a specific task.

## The improvement loop (cockpit and self-improvement)

- **Cockpit (weekly, with the end-of-week review):** run `os-activity-review` to render your cockpit from the logs: what marketing ran, drafts waiting on you, what stalled, estimated time saved.
- **Self-improvement (monthly):** marketing is a fast-moving domain (platform behaviour and algorithms shift), so run `os-self-improvement` on this department about monthly, or sooner for algorithm-driven skills. It reviews the logs, your edits and declines, and fresh research, then proposes skill updates for your approval. Never applied without your yes, always backed up first.
