## What This Is

This is your personalised AI operating system: a set of markdown files that teach any AI assistant to work like you. Your voice. Your audience. Your rules. Your business context.

It's not a chatbot. It's not an app. It's a portable, platform-neutral system that works with whatever AI tools you already use, whether Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, or anything else.

---

## The 6 Components

Your AI operating system is built from exactly 6 components:

| # | Component | File(s) | What It Does |
|---|-----------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | **The Brain** | `AGENT.md` | Your business context, brand voice, rules, and ethics. The master instruction set. |
| 2 | **The Skills** | `skills/*.md` | One file per task. Each skill teaches the AI how to do a specific job. |
| 3 | **The Memory** | `memory/*.md` | Persistent context that accumulates over time. Content logs, performance data, audience insights. |
| 4 | **The Connections** | `connections.md` | Your tech stack mapped to AI integration points. |
| 5 | **The Rules** | `governance.md` | What the AI can do automatically vs what needs your approval. Safety boundaries. |
| 6 | **The Rhythm** | `schedule.md` | When things happen. Daily, weekly, monthly automations. |

## The three levels

There are three ways to run this, and the files are organised to match. Start at Level 1 today and move up when you are ready. See `QUICK_START.md` for the step-by-step.

### Level 1: Skills only
- **skills/**: each skill is a custom bot you can use straight away (a custom GPT, a Gem, a Copilot agent, or a Claude skill)
- **skills/brand-voice.md**: so everything sounds like you
- **QUICK_START.md**: how to set it up

Works on any AI, including the free tiers. Paste a skill into a custom bot and use it this week.

### Level 2: Skills + Schedule
Everything from Level 1, plus:
- **AGENT.md**: your project instructions, the standing brief
- **connections.md**: the tools to connect
- **governance.md**: auto vs ask rules
- **schedule.md**: when things run
- **memory/**: calendar and audience insights

Runs on a paid AI assistant (ChatGPT with Tasks, Claude with Cowork) on a rhythm, reading from and writing to your real tools.

### Level 3: Full agentic OS
Everything from Level 2, plus:
- **org-chart.md**: your AI department structure
- **README.md**: the setup guide your agent reads first

The whole department as a folder on your computer. Point Claude Code, Codex, or OpenClaw at it and tell it to read the README. It runs itself, inside the autonomy and governance you set.

## How to use

1. Read `QUICK_START.md` for the three levels and platform-specific setup.
2. At Level 1, stand up one skill as a custom bot. At Level 2, add `AGENT.md`, connections, and the schedule. At Level 3, run the whole folder with an agentic coding tool.
3. Reference skill files when you need the AI to do specific tasks.
