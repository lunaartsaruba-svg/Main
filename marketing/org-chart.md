# Org Chart: Luna Arts and Sensory

Your AI marketing department, mapped: who reports to whom, and how a request flows through to a finished, on-brand, approved piece.

## You: the CEO

Lugbelkis Wernet: strategy, decisions, and final approval. The AI works for you, to the rules you set.

## The brain

- **`AGENT.md`**: the master instruction set. It reads your context and voice, then routes each request to the right skill.

## The marketing team (skills)

Each skill is a specialist that does one job to a standard, in your voice. You get the ones your answers call for:

- **Strategy:** `skills/marketing-strategy.md`, `skills/content-strategy.md`, `skills/campaign-strategy.md`
- **Voice and craft:** `skills/brand-voice.md`, `skills/content-formula.md`
- **Per platform:** `skills/linkedin-voice.md`, `skills/instagram-strategy.md`, `skills/youtube-strategy.md`, `skills/tiktok-strategy.md`, `skills/carousel-format.md`, `skills/video-scripts.md`, `skills/platform-playbook.md`
- **Owned channels:** `skills/blog-seo-guide.md`, `skills/email-marketing.md`, `skills/email-sequences.md`, `skills/newsletter-format.md`, `skills/newsletter-to-social.md`
- **Conversion:** `skills/dm-conversation-scripts.md`, `skills/sales-page-copy.md`

## Shared memory

- **Editorial Calendar:** `memory/calendar.md`
- **Audience Insights:** `memory/audience-insights.md`

## The layers underneath

- **Connections:** what the team can reach, in `connections.md`
- **Governance:** the rules every output passes, in `governance.md`
- **Rhythm:** when the work happens, in `schedule.md`

## How a request flows

1. You give the brain (`AGENT.md`) a request.
2. The brain picks the right skill.
3. The skill consults your memory (calendar, audience insights) and connections.
4. The output is checked against `governance.md` (voice, the responsible-use floor, the risk tier).
5. It comes to you for approval per the tier in `governance.md`, then publishes.

## Beyond marketing

Marketing is one department. Your Admin & Ops OS, and the departments still rolling out (Sales, Client Delivery, Strategy, Finance & Legal, CEO), are separate operating systems that share the same Foundation, so they already know your voice and rules. Add them when you are ready.
