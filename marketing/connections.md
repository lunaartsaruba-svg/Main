## Content Studio tools (production and scheduling)

The Content Studio turns approved plans into finished assets and queues them through YOUR tools. No paid tool is required: the zero-cost default path is Metricool's free plan for scheduling, Claude-side rendering for brand graphics, and Canva's free tier for carousels. Paid options are named with their price so you can decide; there is no affiliate arrangement behind any of them.

- **Scheduling (default: Metricool):** its official MCP works on every plan including Free, so your AI can queue approved posts at no cost. **Buffer Team** (about $12 per channel per month) is the teams option: its API lands posts as drafts behind Buffer's own approval flow. **Blotato** (about $29 per month) is the power option with the widest platform and format reach; note it has no approval gate of its own, so with Blotato the OS-side approval rule below is the only gate. No scheduler at all? The Studio outputs a paste-ready manual queue, and Buffer's free tier covers three channels.
- **Design (default: your Canva):** Canva's MCP builds multi-slide carousels on any plan (platform resizing needs Canva Pro, a nicety not a dependency). Brand graphics and quote cards render directly from `memory/brand-kit.md` at no cost.
- **AI imagery and video (optional):** Higgsfield via its MCP for photographic images and short b-roll clips, on your own account and credits. Turn OFF "train on my content" in its settings, and note AI-generated outputs may not carry copyright protection.

**The approval rule for everything in this section:** the OS holds every content package until you sign it off. Only approved content is pushed to a scheduler, at draft or queued level wherever the tool supports it. Scheduled means live, so approval always happens first, on the OS side.

Keys and logins stay in your own tools with the least privilege that works. A scheduler API key can usually publish to every connected account, so treat it like a password and never paste it into any OS file.

## Access levels (how much the AI can do per tool)

| Level | What the AI may do | Start here for |
|---|---|---|
| **Read-only** | See and summarise (your calendar, analytics, past posts) | Everything, on day one |
| **Draft** | Create drafts that sit waiting for you (scheduled-but-unpublished posts, email drafts) | Tools you check daily |
| **Send / publish** | Push live on its own | Only once you trust a specific skill, and only within governance |

Raise a tool from read-only to draft to send deliberately, never all at once.

## The rules

- The AI may **read** from any tool listed here.
- The AI may **draft** to any channel, but **never auto-sends or publishes externally** unless you have explicitly raised that tool to send level and the action is within `governance.md`.
- Connect accounts you own. Note who owns each login so access can be changed or revoked cleanly.
- Protect data: the AI uses the least personal information needed, and never exports subscriber or client lists anywhere they should not go.
- Anything not on this list is out of scope. The AI flags it and asks.
