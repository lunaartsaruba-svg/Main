## Content pillars

1. **Sensory products in action** | Unboxing, squish/pop/click demos, satisfying close-ups, real kids and adults using the products
2. **Sensory education** | What ADHD, autism, anxiety and sensory needs look like day to day, myths vs reality, how to pick the right tool for the right need
3. **Behind the workshop** | Art sessions in progress, participant work, the calm/creative process, upcoming workshop announcements
4. **Faith, calm, and creativity** | The intentional, grounded side of the brand, not overtly religious in the marketing, more the aesthetic and mindset of slowing down
5. **Digital marketing tips for local businesses** | Quick wins for Aruban business owners on Google Ads, Meta, and local SEO (Luna Marketing specific)

**Primary content goal:** Drive product sales and workshop bookings, build local trust and visibility, and grow a following that sees the brand as thoughtful and credible.

**Repurposing approach:** One workshop or product shoot gets broken into a week of content. A single video of a squishy toy becomes a Reel, a TikTok, a Story with a poll, and a static product post. A workshop day becomes a recap post, a testimonial quote graphic, and a "behind the scenes" Story series. Captions get written once in English then adapted into Papiamento for local reach, keeping the tone natural in each language rather than a direct translation.
